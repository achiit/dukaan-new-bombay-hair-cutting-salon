My grandfather started with one kadhai

I am Rajesh Sharma. In 1988, my dada ji started this shop with a single kadhai and a coal stove. I still remember sitting on a sack of sugar after school, watching him stir the peda for an hour without once looking away.

My father took over in 2003 and I joined him in 2011. The shop is bigger now, but the recipes have not changed. We still buy our milk from the same three families in nearby villages.

If a sweet is not right, we do not sell it. That is how dada ji did it, and that is how we do it.

  • Only desi ghee, never vanaspati
  • Milk collected fresh every morning
  • Sweets made daily, never stored for days
  • Same-day delivery within Sirsa city
  • Sugar-light options for elders
